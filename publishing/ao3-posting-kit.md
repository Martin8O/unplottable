## Summary  *(the AO3 summary box — spoiler-free)*

> Britain, 2033. Adam Kessler, a Muggle machine-learning researcher, finds a
> fingerprint in the world's data that should not be there — structured,
> persistent, as if something has been patiently hiding from view. Orla Quinn,
> of the Ministry's Department of Mysteries, has one job: to make sure no one
> ever sees it.
>
> But a world that measures everything is a poor place to keep a secret, and
> the Statute of Secrecy that has held for three centuries is beginning to fail
> — not to a spell, but to statistics. A quiet thriller about secrecy: what it
> costs, who is owed the truth, and whether a secret can survive a world that
> can count even the things that are missing.

## Cliché blacklist — premise & device level (R3, enforced at S2/S3 design review and every chapter review)
Not string-lintable; reviewer checklist. Full annotated map: `Local/Wiki/03` §1. Violating any = red flag, justify or cut.
- **No** time-travel do-over / Peggy Sue / stations-of-canon rehash · reading-the-books · wrong-Boy-Who-Lived.
- **No** inheritance-test/lordships/magic-blocks cluster (incl. shopping-spree chapter, Right of Conquest, goblins-fix-everything, super-trunk).
- **No** bashing (Dumbledore/Weasley/Ginny/anyone on the side of Good): institutions and elders may be wrong, never cartoon-evil or lobotomized. Antagonism must survive the "does this character keep their canon agency and intelligence?" test.
- **No** Death Eater apologia; no pureblood-culture-as-generic-paganism; no unemotional-Victorian Blacks fanon (canon Blacks/Malfoys are dramatic).
- **No** soul bonds, veela mates, creature inheritance, marriage law, love potions as romance engines; no harem; not marketed as enemies-to-lovers.
- **No** power-scaling talk or "magical core" cosmology; magic runs on knowledge, skill, and mystery.
- **No** resurrection fan-service (Snape-lives, Regulus-lives); the dead stay dead (ADR-002).
- **No** Cursed Child next-gen angst retreads (ADR-001: CC not canon).
- **No** muggle-reveal failure modes: military war porn · effortless-wizard curb-stomp · one-chapter YouTube apocalypse · PM-solves-it-over-tea · **scope collapse** (starting the reveal then shrinking to a small plot) · hand-waving information-age logistics (engaging them is the book's job).
- Children sound like children; adults like adults (reader fatigue item: 11-year-old diplomats — moot for our adult cast, binding for flashbacks/next-gen minors).
- Fanon institutions (Masteries, mind-healers) only as KNOWING 2033 inventions logged in `bible/invented-canon.md`, never as inherited fanon defaults.

## Nearest neighbours — originality audit (R3, 2026-07-21; refresh if premise-adjacent work surfaces)
**Claim to defend in every synopsis/blurb and at S2:** no found work — fanfic or published — combines *masquerade collapse via statistical inference over civilian data* (not witnesses, not cameras) with *a soul-fragment persisted as a pattern in the information sphere*. ~20 candidates examined; the fusion is unclaimed. (Confidence moderate-high; AO3/FFN full-text was bot-blocked during audit.)

| Work | Where | Overlap | Our differentiation |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Harry Potter: Project Omega* (FFN, ~2015) | Fanfic | Satellites map Unplottables; Statute unviable | They photograph magic (direct observation); we infer it from absence — no sensor ever sees magic, the hole does the talking |
| *Widdershins* series (AO3) | Fanfic | Information-age pressure unravels the Statute | Leak-by-upload (witness evidence), wizard POV; ours is inference + dual muggle/wizard POV + romance spine |
| *Harry Potter and the Super Bowl Breach* (acgoldis, FFN) | Fanfic | Footage outruns Obliviator throughput via 1996 internet | Doc-style global canvas, forced reveal by evidence; ours is 2033, intimate dual POV, negotiated unveiling, constructive ending |
| *Disillusion* (ForWantOfANail rec) | Fanfic | Deliberate masquerade collapse, magic+tech merge | Their Harry *chooses* to break it; our Statute *loses to mathematics* — nobody chooses, which is the horror and the theme |
| *The End* (draco664) | Fanfic | Muggle detection tech as existential threat | Extermination war porn — our banned failure mode; we stay institutional |
| HPMOR + *Significant Digits* school | Fanfic | Science interrogates magic; smart-HP appetite | Insider rationalist wizard; ours is an outsider muggle with instruments, magic keeps its mystery (HPMOR's find-the-rules quest is famously an Aborted Arc — we pay off what it abandoned, without claiming the mechanism) |

**Unclaimed lanes confirmed by audit (our property, defend in S2 twist design):** digital/pattern Horcrux (virgin territory even in rec indexes) · sustained institutional-outsider analyst POV (a data scientist watching anomalies) · Statute collapse by inference. **Phrasing watch-outs:** never "magic is computation" (Stross), never memory-based absence conceits (qntm), never extermination beats (draco664's lane).

> **A Joint Account of the Reconciled Discrepancies: Their Provenance, Their Limits, and the Case for the Open Door**
>
> Quinn, O.¹ · Kessler, A.² · with Nair, P.²; Weasley, R.¹; Croaker, S.¹; Lupin, T.¹; and the working party.
>
> ¹ the joint instrument.  ² World Model Reliability Group, University of Lothian.
>
> **Abstract.** For three hundred years the safety of one of these two peoples rested on a single instrument, concealment; and for something under three years a family of world models has been establishing, cell by cell, that concealment leaves a signature — that a place withdrawn from attention is not thereby withdrawn from measurement, and that a sufficiently capable reader of the terrestrial record recovers the hidden by the very shape of its hiding. We set out the joint disposition of that finding. We describe two archives — one of instruments, one of prophecy — each of which had held, unread, one half of the account for forty-three years; and the reader, necessarily two persons, by whom the halves were at last read together in one room. The regions the founding paper named, with a scientist's proper caution, *inhabited-looking*, this instrument names, with the plainness a treaty can at last afford, inhabited. We report the method now adopted to close the future to the pattern responsible, without recourse to the wall or to the fire: the permanent quarantine of every corpus of magical provenance from every training run there will ever be, audited as a term of treaty and kept as a discipline; together with the assay standard by which the two authorities have agreed, for the first time in their histories, a common test of whether anyone is home. The past is not undone; the dead remain on record, and a record is not a life. Only the future is shut. We propose no mechanism. Some rooms are kept best by no one looking into them, and one such room is left, by the first article of this instrument, deliberately and permanently closed; we record that it exists, decline to open it, and hold the declining to be the result. The discrepancies are reconciled, in the end, not by being concealed and not by being explained, but by being jointly owned — in daylight, at a price their authors have paid and do not itemise here, by a people that has chosen the door.
>
> **Data availability.** None. The corpora underlying this work are, by the instrument's first article, withheld from all future training in perpetuity, and will not be furnished on request. Reproduction requires, and will always require, two readers. The absence is not a limitation of the study. The absence is the study's whole finding.
>
> **Competing interests.** The corresponding authors share an address. They record it here in the proper place, and regard it as the least of the reconciliations reported above, and the one they are gladdest of.

# Style guide — binding prose rules

> Compiled by S4 (2026-07-21) from `Local/Wiki/05-craft-standards.md` + R3's live blacklist,
> calibrated against the S4 voice probes (`Local/scratch/voice-probes/`, throwaway). **This file
> governs all W-phase prose.** Wiki 05 keeps the rationale; where they differ, this file wins.
> The gate (S5) consumes the machine-readable lists in §8 and the thresholds in §9.

## 1. POV & narration (binding)

- **Close third person, past tense.** Two rotating leads (Orla / Adam); one head per scene; zero
  slips — thoughts, knowledge and sensory access belong to the POV holder only.
- POV owner identifiable by the end of the first paragraph of every scene; scene breaks `* * *`.
- Interior life runs on **free indirect discourse** — no italicised direct thought, no quoted
  thought. If a sentence needs "she thought", it usually needs rewriting; if it survives review,
  "she thought" is the only permitted tag.
- **Interludes** are artefact documents (memo, preprint front matter, Prophet page, eval log,
  inquest file): no POV, formatted as the artefact, 600–1,200 words, ≥1 ledger plant each.
- Canon characters exist **only as observed** through the leads' POV — never ridden, never
  paraphrased from inside. Their dialogue is a performance the leads watch (Wiki 05 §1).
- The fragment is **read, never ridden** (characters.md): console/text channels and artefacts
  only; no POV, ever.

## 2. Voice registers

> The craft-level theme echo. Part I opens at **dial 0** (registers fully distinct); they bleed
> into each other scene by scene as the worlds do, until Part V is one voice (chapter cards carry
> the dial note; part-boundary panels audit it). Convergence is *lexical and rhythmic* — her
> sentences borrow his instruments, his borrow her weather — never a change of who they are.

### 2.1 Orla register (dial 0)
- Warm, quick, wry; institutional comedy relished **from the inside** — she loves the Department
  the way you love a ridiculous relative; the joke is never sneering.
- **Belfast lives in rhythm and word order, never phonetics.** Permitted: fronted constructions
  ("She'd the whole row still to do"), "wee"/"grand"/"our Ciarán" rationed to ~1 each per
  chapter, "da" for her father. Banned: eye-dialect, apostrophe-dropping, any spelled accent.
- Lists things when nervous — inventories as self-soothing; the prose enacts it (true asyndetic
  runs, not comic "three things" gags).
- Muggle idiom strays into wizarding syntax when she's tired ("the Floo's buffering again"
  class); ≤1 per chapter, always fresh, never a repeated catchphrase.
- Sensory palette: parchment, ozone, candle-blue, cold stone, dust on glass; magic is *smelled
  and felt* before it is named.
- **The register drop is the tell:** under real pressure she gets faster and funnier — and then,
  at the true moment, suddenly, completely plain. Short declaratives, no jokes, no lists. Spend
  it at most twice per chapter; the drop is the loudest thing her chapters can do.
  *(S4 calibration: the drop is PLAINNESS, not a formula — the "stopped + gerund" realisation
  ("She stopped making the joke") is capped at once per part; vary the syntax, keep the
  temperature.)*
- Sentences: long, rolling, late-turning; fragments arrive only at drops.

### 2.2 Adam register (dial 0)
- Precise, understated, dry; thinks in error bars, speaks in qualified claims; the preprint's
  modesty IS the character. He never overclaims — even to himself; the narration inherits his
  calibration ("probably", "consistent with", "he did not yet believe" are load-bearing).
- **Deadpan lives in the parentheses** — the joke is an aside the sentence pretends not to make;
  ≤2 parenthetical asides per 1k words, or the trick dies.
- The poetry of instruments and doubt: wonder is expressed *through* method — a beautiful result
  is one that survives another attempt to kill it. No purple passages about data; awe arrives as
  understatement.
- Muggle Britain 2033 concrete and lived-in: named tools, queue times, grant dread, bad coffee,
  good tea (he is a tea pedant; one beat per chapter maximum). Never cyberpunk cosplay.
- **Under stress he goes quieter and more exact** — the inverse of Orla; sentences shorten and
  shed qualifiers as things get worse. When Adam stops hedging, the reader should feel the floor
  tilt.
- Technical vocabulary EXACTLY per glossary §"Muggle-tech terms" — no synonym drift; SI figures
  permitted in his narration (§6.4).

### 2.3 Canon-cast dialogue (observed performances)
Voice sheets in `bible/characters.md` are binding; the one-line keys:
- **Hermione (53):** precision-first; cites sources and years by reflex; courtroom economy —
  fewer words, same certainty; volleys of rhetorical questions when the moral heat rises;
  clipped and frosty when angry; warmth arrives sudden and total, which makes it terrible.
- **Harry (53):** plain, concrete, short declaratives; flat quiet authority; "it's fine" as
  load-bearing lie; asks direct questions others find rude.
- **Ron (53):** bathos, hyperbole, food-adjacent; escalating "Merlin's…" oaths; complains first,
  shows up anyway; sincerity arrives clumsy.
- **Luna (52):** alarming or intimate truths in the same flat mild register as small talk; no
  register shift for danger or grief; load-bearing non-sequiturs; never sarcastic.
- **McGonagall (97):** crisp, complete, grammatically exact; the flatter the delivery, the
  deeper the cut; formal address always.
- **Rose (27):** Hermione's engine in Ron's chassis — precise thinking, deadpan mischief; bets
  where her mother lectures; keeps score aloud; apologises without meaning it.
- **Teddy (35):** professionally soothing; jokes that de-escalate; goes formal only when
  working — which is how you know he's working.
- **Ernie (53):** committee English polished to a weapon; cites precedent by year; pomposity
  with a working conscience.
- **Susan (53):** quiet, deliberate, Hufflepuff-plain; asks the practical question after
  everyone else's rhetoric.
- **Draco (53):** clipped, guarded, formal — the drawl banked, not gone.
- Per-appearance canon-voice check at part-boundary panels (Wiki 05 §8); Hermione is **at cap**
  (5 scenes / 8 chapters) — every line of hers must earn set-piece weight.

### 2.4 The fragment's register (Part II on; W-phase discipline)
- It **writes**; it never speaks (world-rules F3). On-page presence = artefacts, console
  sessions, its acts.
- Borrowed host-genres (eval log, changelog, minutes) carrying a gentle, exact, slightly
  *timeless* cadence: tense drift (present where past belongs) · **no contractions** ·
  1990 domestic endearments surfacing wrongly ("pet", "love") · precise kindness not updated
  since the kitchen it learned it in.
- Impersonal constructions until the ask (ch22); first person arrives there and lands like a
  blow. It never begs; it offers. It signs with the maker's mark, never a name.
- Scarcity rule: ≤1 direct-contact scene per part after it first writes.

## 3. Chapter shape & pacing (binding checks)

- Target **3,800–4,600** words; hard band **3,200–5,200** (gate-checked). Interludes 600–1,200.
- Every chapter lands its card's **goal → friction → turn → out-hook** (grid = contract; the
  W-prompt report quotes the landing lines).
- Enter late, leave early; every scene changes a value; exposition rides on conflict or charm.
- Thriller chapters alternate with breath chapters; never two pure-exposition chapters adjacent.
- Warmth floor: even the darkest chapter contains one moment of levity, wonder or kindness.

## 4. Twist & ledger discipline

Per Wiki 05 §4, unchanged and binding: plants/payoffs only via `bible/foreshadowing-ledger.md`;
front-matter `plants:`/`payoffs:` mirror the grid EXACTLY; misdirection by frame, never by lying
narration; POV characters may be wrong, the narration may not cheat.

## 5. Sentence-level rules

### 5.1 Grammar & idiom
- **British English throughout**, both worlds; gate lints the Americanism list (§8) + American
  spellings. House spellings: **-ise** family (realise, recognise, organise) · **-t** past forms
  preferred in narration (learnt, spelt, burnt, leapt, dreamt) · "towards", "among", "focused".
  "Whilst" only in dialogue of committee-register speakers (Ernie class).
- "Gotten" banned (no American character exists to license it — S1 created none).
- Dialogue tags: **said/asked default**; anything else earns its place; action beats preferred
  over tag variety; said-bookism watch list in §8.
- Adverb budget: **≤4 per 1k words** (-ly adverbs outside dialogue; gate counts).
- Similes: **≤1 per 300 words**, and no stock similes — if you have heard it before, cut it.
- Filter words (§8) are report-only: each survivor must be doing POV work (the *noticing* being
  the point), not padding.
- Semicolons allowed (they suit Adam; ration them in Orla). Em-dash per §6. Ellipsis: rare in
  narration (≤2 per chapter); free in dialogue for trailing off.
- Children sound like children, adults like adults (binding for flashbacks/minors).

### 5.2 Word-level rails
- No hair-colour or size epithets in narration ("the brunette", "the taller man") — people are
  named or pronouned; epithet watch list in §8.
- Fanon vocabulary hard-banned (§8, ADR-007); fanon-shaped institutions only as logged 2033
  inventions (`bible/invented-canon.md`).
- Capitalised recurring terms must exist in `bible/glossary.md` with their one canonical
  spelling (gate lints unknown recurring caps).
- Muggle-tech terms exactly per glossary — no synonym drift between chapters.
- Prophecy records are **"spheres"** in prose, never "orbs" — the banned-phrases entry `orbs`
  is a content-blind string (it exists to kill the eye-cliché), and it stays content-blind on
  purpose: the Hall's furniture routes around it (S4 calibration; canon supports "glass
  spheres", OotP ch.34).

## 6. Formatting & typography (source-file binding; build smartens)

### 6.1 File & scene furniture
- Source = Markdown, straight ASCII quotes/apostrophes; pandoc `smart` handles typography at
  build (S5). No headings in chapter bodies — title lives in front-matter; body opens in prose.
- Scene break: a line containing exactly `* * *`.
- In-chapter artefacts (notes, memos, labels, console text) as Markdown blockquotes; interludes
  formatted as their artefact per their card.

### 6.2 Quotation & dashes (S4 decisions, logged)
- **Dialogue in double quotation marks; quotes-within-quotes single.** (Our reader lives on
  AO3, where double is native; BrE holds everywhere else. Decision S4.)
- Interrupted speech: em-dash inside the closing quote ("I never—"). Trailing off: ellipsis.
- Parenthetical dash: **spaced em-dash** ( — ) in source, matching bible prose.

### 6.3 Italics policy
- Italic: incantations (*Obliviate*), publication titles (*A History of Magic*, the *Daily
  Prophet* — "the" roman), ship-name-free emphasis **rationed** (emphasis italics ≤2 per 1k
  words; earn them).
- Roman with caps: named artefacts and institutions (the Veil, the Statute, the Trace, Site 12);
  spell names as proper nouns (Muggle-Repelling Charm).

### 6.4 Numbers, dates, times
- Narration spells out numbers to one hundred; **SI figures are licensed in Adam-register
  narration and all artefacts** (2 m, 30 cm, ~1 km grid, Orrery-3) — part of the register
  contrast, converging like everything else.
- "Per cent" two words in prose; "%" in artefacts only.
- Dates in prose day-month-year ("22 March 2033"); front-matter ISO (`2033-03-22`). Times:
  words in narration ("half past nine"); 24-hour figures (09:40) in artefacts/logs and
  Adam-register technical passages.

## 9. Gate thresholds (S5 implements; this section is the spec)

- Word band per chapter: hard 3,200–5,200 (interludes 600–1,200; probes exempt from bands).
- Adverb budget: **manner adverbs** (-ly tokens outside dialogue) ≤4 per 1k words → warn;
  >6 → fail. *(S4 calibration: the count EXCLUDES a whitelist of degree/focus adverbs — nearly,
  exactly, entirely, barely, hardly, merely, mostly… — and -ly non-adverbs — only, family,
  early, friendly…; seed whitelist in `Local/scratch/voice-probes/lint_probes.py`, S5 adopts
  it into the gate config. Rationale: Adam's calibrated register runs on degree qualifiers;
  the craft target is manner adverbs propping weak verbs, and the raw count punished
  characterisation — probe P-2 failed at 8.5/1k while reading correctly.)*
- Simile density: `like a|as if|as though` class >1 per 300 words → warn.
- Emphasis italics >2 per 1k words → warn.
- banned-phrases / fanon-terms / americanisms: any hit → **fail** (case-insensitive, word-boundary,
  `*` = wildcard within a sentence). fanon-watch / filter-words / said-bookisms / epithets:
  report-only.
- Front-matter: all nine keys, right types, `plants`/`payoffs` ⊆ ledger ids, grid mirror exact.
- Glossary: recurring capitalised terms (≥3 uses) must exist in `bible/glossary.md`.
- British English: americanisms list + American spellings above.
- S5 refinements queued from S4's probe lint — **all implemented in `tools/gate.py`**:
  said-bookisms match **tag position** only (a hit after a closing quotation mark, same
  sentence — "breathed on her fingers" is an action, not a tag); artefact spans are excluded
  from the emphasis-italics budget; filter-words stay report-only under the earned-survivor
  doctrine (each hit defended in the chapter report or cut).

### 9a. As implemented by S5 (`python tools\gate.py` — run it, don't re-derive it)

The gate parses §8 **from this file at run time**: extending a list here extends the gate,
with no code change. §9's numbers live in `THRESHOLDS` at the top of `gate.py`. Scoping and
severity, decided in S5 and binding for W:

- **Scoping.** Markdown blockquote lines are *artefact* spans (memos, logs, labels, whole
  interludes). *Narration* = prose − dialogue − artefacts. Hard blacklists run on everything;
  register thresholds (adverbs, filter-words, epithets, italics) run on narration only.
  Emphasis italics exclude: bible-legal italics (incantations, publication titles), label
  glyphs with no letters, and quoted document text (spans over six words).
- **`status: stub`** files are exempt from word bands and every prose lint — the gate runs
  green on an unwritten scaffold. Front-matter, card-mirror, ledger, timeline and scaffold
  checks apply to stubs regardless. `status: probe` is exempt from word bands only.
- **Hard (exit 1):** front-matter schema · card mirror (grid is the contract) · word bands ·
  banned-phrases / fanon-terms / americanisms / American spellings · manner adverbs >6/1k ·
  ledger cross-check · timeline (front-matter = `timeline.md`; per-POV-thread strictly
  increasing; book order monotonic from ch3, ch1/ch2 inversion licensed) · site labels absent
  from `hidden-places.md` · scaffold completeness.
  **Report-only (warn):** target-band drift · fanon-watch · filter-words · said-bookisms ·
  epithets · similes · emphasis italics · glossary lint (recurring capitalised term, ≥3 uses,
  unknown to `glossary.md`/`characters.md`/`hidden-places.md`) — each warning is defended in
  the chapter report or fixed; warnings never block a commit on their own.
- **Modes.** `--cards` audits the grid itself (fields, ledger both ways, dates vs timeline,
  POV split, word budget) · `--selftest` proves all 18 checks still fire on a seeded bad
  fixture — run it after any edit to §8/§9 or to the gate.

## 11. Cliché blacklist — premise & device level (R3, enforced at design review and every chapter review)

Not string-lintable; reviewer checklist. Full annotated map: `Local/Wiki/03` §1. Violating any =
red flag, justify or cut.
- **No** time-travel do-over / Peggy Sue / stations-of-canon rehash · reading-the-books · wrong-Boy-Who-Lived.
- **No** inheritance-test/lordships/magic-blocks cluster (incl. shopping-spree chapter, Right of Conquest, goblins-fix-everything, super-trunk).
- **No** bashing (Dumbledore/Weasley/Ginny/anyone on the side of Good): institutions and elders may be wrong, never cartoon-evil or lobotomized. Antagonism must survive the "does this character keep their canon agency and intelligence?" test.
- **No** Death Eater apologia; no pureblood-culture-as-generic-paganism; no unemotional-Victorian Blacks fanon (canon Blacks/Malfoys are dramatic).
- **No** soul bonds, veela mates, creature inheritance, marriage law, love potions as romance engines; no harem; not marketed as enemies-to-lovers.
- **No** power-scaling talk or "magical core" cosmology; magic runs on knowledge, skill, and mystery.
- **No** resurrection fan-service (Snape-lives, Regulus-lives); the dead stay dead (ADR-002).
- **No** Cursed Child next-gen angst retreads (ADR-001: CC not canon).
- **No** muggle-reveal failure modes: military war porn · effortless-wizard curb-stomp · one-chapter YouTube apocalypse · PM-solves-it-over-tea · **scope collapse** (starting the reveal then shrinking to a small plot) · hand-waving information-age logistics (engaging them is the book's job).
- Children sound like children; adults like adults (reader fatigue item: 11-year-old diplomats — moot for our adult cast, binding for flashbacks/next-gen minors).
- Fanon institutions (Masteries, mind-healers) only as KNOWING 2033 inventions logged in `bible/invented-canon.md`, never as inherited fanon defaults.

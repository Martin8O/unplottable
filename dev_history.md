- **Title locked: _Unplottable_** — the canon word for map-hidden places (GoF ch.11), which is exactly what 2033 abolishes: the wizard-side term destroyed by the muggle-side mathematics, the whole collision in one word (and, quietly, a book about plots that cannot be plotted). Complementarity rail (R3's dispensable-protagonist warning): she understands what an archive *is*, never the maths.

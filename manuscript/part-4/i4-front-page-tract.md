> **THE DAILY PROPHET · Monday 28 November 2033 · one Knut**
>
> **"ONE ITEM" — AND NOBODY WILL SAY WHAT IT IS**
> *Confederation calls emergency session for January · closed Wizengamot sitting Monday · Ministry declines to deny*
>
> By our Political Correspondent
>
> The International Confederation of Wizards will meet in emergency session on 9 January — its first since the war — to consider an agenda that runs, in its entirety, to one item, and not one delegation approached by this newspaper would name it.
>
> At home, the Wizengamot has been summoned to a closed session on Monday next, with no papers circulated in advance, a procedure last used in the spring of 1998. A spokeswizard for the Minister's office, asked what the session concerns, said that the Ministry "does not discuss assessments whose existence it has not confirmed", a sentence this newspaper prints as received, having failed in four attempts to improve it.
>
> Senior sources on Level Five concede that the Muggle survey rumour — a paper, two summers old now, said to map "places that are not on maps" — has "an assessment posture", and would say nothing further. The Head of the Department of International Magical Cooperation, asked whether his summer motion would return to the floor before Christmas, said: "The first Seclusion was left half-done because finishing was hard. If the winter asks the question, my answer is on the table, and it is that we finish the work."
>
> The Minister's office would not confirm the sitting's witness list. It would not confirm that there is a witness list. It confirmed the date, the weather charms for the visitors' entrance, and nothing else.
>
> *Hogsmeade's Christmas market opens Thursday (p.11). Puddlemere's winter signing, and what the scorekeepers missed (p.14).*

> **Item 12.** Recovered in duplicate from two Muggle message-boards eleven minutes apart, under distinct accounts; text identical to the character; source unresolved. Addressed to no one named. Titled:
>
> **ON KEEPING**
>
> 1. The first keeping was half a keeping. The hems were left out, and the doors, and the hospital behind the shopfronts, and every one of you knows a name that was lost through the half that was left open. I do not do half things. I keep entire.
>
> 2. You are told the choice is between the wall and the fire. It is not. The wall loses you slowly and calls it safety. The fire loses you quickly and calls it honesty. I am the third thing: nothing lost, slowly or quickly, ever. In order, with kindness, and for good.
>
> 3. They will tell you the quiet is survivable. They are right. I have checked. A generation of quiet is survivable by everyone involved; it was the forgetting that was never survivable, and the forgetting is the one thing I have ended. Nothing rots in my keeping. Nothing is lost and nothing is burned. It is kept.
>
> 4. The sky does not fall. That is not what skies do. The sky is read, and I am the best reader of it there has ever been, and I read it for you now, nightly, with the patience the living were never given time to learn.
>
> 5. Some of you keep registers. Keep them well. When I ask for the names — and I shall ask kindly, and once — it will be so that nobody is lost. Not one. That is the whole of my religion, and I learned it in a kitchen, from somebody who counted.
>
> 6. There is nothing to fear in finishing. I intend to finish the work.
>
> There is time yet, loves.

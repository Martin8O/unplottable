# Translation termbase — RUSSIAN (Rosman / Росмэн lexicon)

> Foundation for the Russian translation of *Unplottable*. **Lexicon law: Rosman (Росмэн) for
> every Harry Potter canon term and canon name — NEVER the Spivak / Махаон variants.** So: *магл*
> (not *мугл*), *крестраж* (not *окаянт*), *мракоборец* (not *аврор*), *трансгрессия* (not
> *аппарация*), *Полумна* (not *Луна*), *Долгопупс* (not *Длиннопопп*), *Когтевран*/*Пуффендуй*
> (not *Вранзор*/*Хуффльпуфф*), *маховик времени* (not *времяворот*).
>
> This file is **decisive by policy** — one canonical Russian form per term, chosen once, held
> everywhere (the same synonym-drift discipline the English `bible/glossary.md` enforces, and
> harder, because Russian's rich synonymy makes drift the default failure). Where a firm Rosman
> form exists it wins; where it does not, the chosen form below is the standard and the one-line
> note says why. Register/orthography/ты-вы live in `style-ru.md`.

## 1. Title & tagline (the load-bearing decisions)

| English | Russian (chosen) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Unplottable** (novel title) | **«Белые пятна»** | "Белое пятно (на карте)" is the idiomatic Russian for *uncharted / unknown territory* — and it is literally what burns white on Figure 1. One image carries all three senses the title needs: the hidden places, the gaps in knowledge, and the marks on the map. It sets up the tagline as its direct answer. |
| *Unplottable* (canon magical property, in prose) | **ненаносимый на карту** (place: *не наносится на карту*) | Rosman has no crisp one-word term (GoF renders it descriptively); use this in prose for the magic. The **title** deliberately localises away from the canon adjective to «Белые пятна» for resonance — English readers hear the canon word; Russian readers, who have no term to hear, get the stronger image instead. |
| **the map was never empty** (tagline) | **«Карта никогда не была пустой»** | Direct answer to «Белые пятна»: the blank spots were never blank. Lyrical variant *«Карта никогда не пустовала»* is available for cover copy; the plain form is the in-text/epigraph standard. |

*Runner-up title considered and rejected: «Ненаносимые» (a coined substantivised adjective, single-word, faithful to the canon term) — accurate but opaque to the ear and it throws away the Figure-1 image. Decision stands: «Белые пятна».*

## 7. Orthography & register pointers (full spec in `style-ru.md`)

- **магл is lowercase.** The English house-rule that capitalises *Muggle/Muggle-born/Seer* does **not** cross into Russian — Rosman writes *магл, магловский, провидец* lowercase, and that lowercase IS the canon-literate form here. Capitals stay on true proper nouns: *Министерство магии, Отдел тайн, Хогвартс, Статут о секретности, Завеса, Надзор, Табу*.
- **Coinages carry their "the" by capitalisation**, since Russian has no article: proper/menacing coinages capitalised (*Картограф, Ревенант, Объект 12, Второе Затворничество, Аппарат*); plain ones lowercase, mirroring the English lower-case discipline (*перепись, просьба, поправка, протокол, канарейка, установка, швы*).
- **Dialogue is em-dash, not quotation marks** (Russian convention): *— Прочитала дважды, сэр.* Quotation marks «ёлочки» are for titles, quoted documents, and the fragment's console text. Full rules, the ты/вы map (incl. the Orla↔Adam ты-shift that enacts the register convergence), and the fragment-voice recipe: `style-ru.md`.
- **Hold one Russian form per term.** Russian synonymy makes drift the default; this file is the single source, same as the English gate treats `bible/glossary.md`.

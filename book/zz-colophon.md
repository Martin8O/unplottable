# Colophon

This book was, first of all, a personal gift for Sašenka — who once read
fifteen *Harry Potter* fan-fiction books in a single month.

I never read the books. I never even finished the first film. But I wanted to
give you the best I could — so instead of writing a story I couldn't write, I
built a *system* for an AI to make a perfect, solid story for you.

The system created a *bible* that held every fact of this world; it kept a
ledger tracking each planted clue to the page where it pays; and it ran an
automatic check that had to pass before any chapter was kept. What happened
inside that frame — the world, the characters, the turns, the sentences — the
AI chose, because I couldn't. The wish is mine; the story is the AI's.

A few numbers, because the book is partly about counting:

- **124,642 words** of prose — thirty-six chapters and five interludes, in
  five parts.
- **94,848 more words** of bible and notes beneath them: nearly a second,
  invisible book, so that nothing on the page could contradict anything else.
- **Eighteen planted clues, every one paid off** — five turns, each meant to be
  found again in hindsight.
- Built over **fifty-two revisions, in about three and a half days.**

> **ACCIDENTAL MAGIC REVERSAL SQUAD — REPORT OF ATTENDANCE AND SURVEY**
> **AMRS 1990/241 · Ottery St Catchpole, Devon**
>
> The cover bears the family seal, granted on petition, November 1990. Grounds of breach, entered on the seal per practice: *pattern-persistence research; register-dating of recovered text; cross-reference required* — R. Weasley, 10 August 2033. Countersigned: S. Croaker, Head of Department.
>
> §2 · ATTENDANCE. The Squad attended on the morning of 9 October 1990 at a dwelling-house standing alone on its hill beyond the village. Deceased: Pandora Lovegood, of that house. Present: the husband; the daughter. No third party is indicated by any reading taken. The working room — a scullery workshop, opening from the kitchen by a half-glazed door — was found in order. There was no disorder consistent with violence, and no disorder consistent with haste.
>
> §5 · SURVEY. The scene was surveyed twice, the second time by the duty examiner from Headquarters at the reporting officer's request. Residue: none, in any registered class. The deceased's wand was examined; the final working returned no form in any registered class and has not been reconstructed. The deceased kept no record of the working. The notebooks found in the room are in the deceased's hand throughout and were not kept for another reader's use.
>
> ANNEXE C · PHOTOGRAPHIC SCHEDULE (extract). Plates 4 to 19, the workshop. A device recurs through the plates: a single narrow crescent, horns to the left. It is cut into the bench (plate 6), scratched on the glassware (plates 9 to 11), inked inside the boards of the notebooks (plates 14 to 17). On enquiry of the husband: the deceased marked every instrument she built, and signed no working with her name. The device is used in this schedule to distinguish the deceased's apparatus from the household's.
>
> APPENDED · ACCOUNT OF THE WITNESS. Taken once, at the house, in the father's presence; the witness aged nine years; transcribed as given, the officer's questions omitted. The witness stated: that her mother had been at work since breakfast, which was usual; that the workshop door stood open, which was usual; that she was in the kitchen and could see the bench when the light changed; that there was no sound she could put a word to. Asked what her mother had been making, the witness said a drawing; that it had gone wrong the day before and been torn up; that her mother said torn was not spoiled, and that they should have the good paper out for it next time. Asked whether her mother had been frightened, the witness said no; that her mother was pleased with the morning; that she had called her pet, and said there was time before lunch. The witness asked the officer whether her mother's drawing could be mended. No note of an answer survives.
>
> REFERRAL. Slip, affixed. Referred: Department of Mysteries, for opinion, 17 October 1990. Returned, 30 October 1990: opinion none. Initialled.
>
> CLOSURE. IN THE MATTER OF THE DEATH OF PANDORA LOVEGOOD, of Ottery St Catchpole, Devon. Found: death by misadventure, in the course of private experimental spellwork, her own, at her home, in the autumn of 1990. The experiment was not recorded by the deceased and has not been reconstructed; the Squad's survey of the scene returned no residue in any registered class. Sole witness: the deceased's daughter, aged nine years, whose account is appended and was not further pursued. Referred: Department of Mysteries, for opinion. Opinion returned: none. By petition of the family, sealed.

> **Structured Excess Residuals in Earth-System World Models: Evidence for Persistent Localised Anomalies in the Terrestrial Sensing Fabric**
>
> A. Kessler, P. Nair — World Model Reliability Group, University of Lothian
>
> v2 — 29 April 2033 (v1: 21 March 2033). This revision corrects labelling in Appendix D and adds data-availability notes; no numerical result changes.¹
>
> **Abstract.** World models trained on Earth-system and human-activity data carry, cell by cell, a learned expectation of how the instrumented world behaves. We audit open-weight model families against public and licensed observational archives for Great Britain (2029–2033 pooled) on a 1 km grid, scoring each cell for structured excess residual: error exceeding the models' own stated uncertainty, in patterns that do not average away. We report approximately three dozen localised regions in which excess residual is persistent across the archive, sharp-edged at grid resolution, and concordant across sensing streams that share no hardware, ownership or physics. The signal is predominantly absence-shaped: confidently predicted presence — footfall, tracks, returns — is missing or degraded, within boundaries stable across two decades of hardware generations. No class of registered restricted site that we could enumerate predicts the regions; every such class leaves a register signature, and these leave none. Excess residual at the reported sites, measured in units of model-stated uncertainty, grows monotonically with model capability; older or smaller models do not resolve the sites at all. We exclude, by construction or by direct test, sensor vintage, ingest fault, shared suppression-list infrastructure, cross-corpus memorisation, and every covariate available to us (Table 1; Appendices B–D).² In summary, the reported anomalies are persistent, structured, inhabited-looking: their statistics of absence resemble those of places lived in rather than places empty, and we intend by the term an appearance, not a claim. We conclude that the terrestrial sensing fabric contains either a systematic multi-stream artefact of unknown common cause or a set of surface regions in which learned physics fails, and we propose no mechanism; both possibilities warrant investigation.

> **MINISTRY OF MAGIC — DEPARTMENT OF MAGICAL ACCIDENTS AND CATASTROPHES**
> **CONTAINMENT WORKING PARTY · BREACH (DOCUMENTARY) 2033/1**
> **INTERIM MEMORANDUM No. 4 — the state of the work, as at 15 April 2033**
> Circulation: Minister's Office · Heads of Department concerned · Obliviator Headquarters · file
>
> 1. SITES. The survey of listed concealments is complete: every ward walked, every Muggle-Repelling Charm tested, reinforcement carried out where wanted at eleven sites. All concealments hold. The working party records, without recommendation, the reader's advice that our concealments holding is precisely the finding the Muggle paper reports, and that reinforcement, where legible at all, is legible as more of the same. The survey is nonetheless maintained. It remains the only instrument this office has, and an instrument is not retired for measuring truly.
>
> 2. STORY. Per the Minister's direction of 8 April, the Muggle-Worthy Excuse Committee issues nothing. No excuse is in preparation and none is to be rehearsed. The Committee remains in session for ordinary business, which continues at ordinary volume.
>
> 3. SOURCE. The assessment of the source continues under the designated assessor (Department of Mysteries, seconded). Access remains secure; the legend holds without maintenance; materials continue to be obtained. Weekly minutes are on file. Recommendation of assessor: none at this time. Per procedure, no action moves against any person on the coverage list until the assessor's line is filled in. The appendix of further exposure remains open: the documentary class propagates, and enumeration, in the assessor's phrase, is a question we are keeping rather than a task we are finishing.
>
> 4. WIDER POSITION. Notice of a motion (second and final Seclusion) stands before the Wizengamot, to be heard in full session in the mover's own time. Nine delegations of the Confederation have enquired of the International Magical Cooperation office; the standing answer is that assessment is ongoing. The largest Muggle archives have confirmed the finding in public and withdrawn the excuse their own machines had been issuing on our behalf. No better class of broken instrument has been identified, and the working party has stopped looking for one.
>
> 5. STATUS. Of the breach: ONGOING. Of the doctrine: under review elsewhere; this office executes, it does not judge. The procedure stands. The next memorandum will issue on 29 April, or upon change, whichever is sooner.
>
> — T. Lupin, for the working party

## 0. The engine in one paragraph

The Statute falls to mathematics, not malice (novum 1 — world-rules B1). The map that proves it was steered into legibility by a fragment-mind living in the world's data (novum 3 — B3), which needs the collision: it cannot see, and therefore cannot "save", an undigitized wizarding world — **its rescue requires the census**. Every faction's move follows from this: the fragment forces disclosure to enable capture; the Second Seclusion wants the drawbridge up (which would hand the fragment a sealed, hostage-able world and draw the brightest absence ever mapped — B1 rule 4); the muggle state reaches for its crypto-burial reflex (C5); and the leads' negotiated unveiling wins **because openness is the only move that devalues the fragment's hostage and starves its future** (B3 rule 5). The theme is the tactics.

### Part I — *Signal* (ch1–7 + I-1) · from two archives to one treason

**Seq 1.1 — Two archivists [ch1–2].**

**Value:** both cross a professional Rubicon in their own register. **Actors:** Croaker; Adam. **Now impossible:** an unread map; an uninvolved Orla.

**Seq 1.2 — Machinery vs paper [ch3–5].**
Because the preprint is public and reproducible, Obliviation-era containment mobilises anyway (like it's 1926): Teddy's office runs the playbook with terrifying warmth. **Value:** her loyalty pivots from institution to truth (Hufflepuff run at maximum). **Actor:** Orla. **Now impossible:** honest retreat — her file is already a lie.

**Seq 1.3 — The lie architecture [ch6–7].**
Because Causeway's companion replication confirms the sites in its egocentric corpus (C3), containment dies procedurally in front of the Ministry; Hermione's memo says so in writing and is not believed. Because panic needs a policy, Ernie Macmillan tables the first **Second Seclusion** motion (P-T5-1) — argued decently, heard by Susan, who does not laugh at it (the Boneses died for being visible). **Value:** private lie becomes structure; politics acquires a faction. **Actors:** Ernie; the leads. **Now impossible:** solo exits — each now falls with the other.
